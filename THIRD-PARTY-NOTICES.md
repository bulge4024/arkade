Console illustrations are read from the user's installed RetroBat Carbon theme when available. Previously copied local Carbon images are excluded from version control; this repository does not redistribute the theme. Original Carbon attribution and license information can be found in the installed theme and [RetroBat Carbon repository](https://github.com/RetroBat-Official/es-theme-carbon).
